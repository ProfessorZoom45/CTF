Gameplay System

ONE GAME. THREE WAYS TO WIN.

I 💥

CHI KO

Direct Assault

Drain your opponent’s Chi to 0 through combat damage and unblocked attacks. Rapid and decisive.

II ⚔️

7 KILLS

Battlefield Domination

Destroy 7 enemy Catalysts in combat to eliminate their frontline and claim complete battlefield victory.

III 📦

7 EXTRACTIONS

Tactical Control

Capture and extract 7 enemy Catalysts. Overpower defensive lines and remove their key assets from the game.
