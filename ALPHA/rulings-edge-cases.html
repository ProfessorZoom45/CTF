Confirmed rule

End Phase payment checks ownership

To pay for Extract, Rescue, or Destroy 1 Trick, use one Catalyst that you own and control. A newly Spawned Catalyst you own may pay. A Catalyst owned by your opponent cannot pay, even while you control it. Taking control never changes ownership. Pass Turn is free.

YES - your new Catalyst can payYou Spawn a Catalyst from your own deck this turn. At End Phase, you still own and control it, so you may send it to the Void as the payment.
NO - a borrowed Catalyst cannot payYou take control of your opponent's Catalyst until End Phase. Your opponent still owns it, so you cannot Tribute or send it as your End Phase payment before it returns.
Easy memory rule: New is okay. Borrowed is not. For a paid End action, you must both own the Catalyst and control it.

Card cases that still need final approval

These are card-specific wording or implementation conflicts from the deferred ANM audit. Later global rulings may answer part of a case, but each card still needs approved final text and an engine sign-off before certification.

Not rules yet: The decision prompts describe what must be chosen. They do not approve the legacy card name, artwork, wording, or suggested behavior for public release.
ANM-012Reverse Blade Sword Engine confirmation

The text says this Equip is treated as "Sakabato," but the engine needs the exact zones and checks where that alias applies.

Decision needed: Confirm whether the alias is recognized for every Sakabato name requirement without changing the printed name or copying other properties.
ANM-013Amakakuro Ryo No Herimeki Pending

The activation condition says a Himura-named card must be on the field, but it does not say who must control it.

Decision needed: Must the activating player control the face-up Himura card, and should all resulting destruction be recorded as effect destruction with no battle Kills?
ANM-014Zero Degrees Pending

The legacy start-of-Main-Phase window and the duration "for two turns" need one exact online timing rule.

Decision needed: Identify the only legal activation window and the exact two completed player turns covered by the lock.
ANM-001Serpent Pending

The card does not state where it Special Spawns from or how often its five-coin Battle effect can be used.

Decision needed: Confirm the source zone, the exact four-card removal requirement, and whether the coin attempt is once per Battle Phase.
ANM-002Ultimate Price Pending

The player needs a legal deck-selection prompt, plus a deterministic clock for the three-turn attack and effect lock.

Decision needed: Define who chooses the target and exactly when the Spawned Catalyst unlocks.
ANM-003Nolan-The-Great Pending

The offering procedure, Spawn classification, first-turn attack lock, and later direct-Chi permission are mixed together.

Decision needed: Approve each part as a separate procedure so the engine can validate and test it.
ANM-021Reaper - Master Swordsman Pending

"Regardless of requirement" might bypass only a Sword Equip's target restriction, or it might bypass normal Equip rules too.

Decision needed: State exactly which requirements Reaper ignores and whether its printed two-Equip maximum overrides the global capacity rule.
ANM-022Rapier Pending

The card adds piercing Chi damage when Pressure exceeds a defending Catalyst's Counter Pressure.

Decision needed: Confirm the order of normal battle, Capture or destruction, Kill credit, and the positive Pressure difference dealt to Chi.
ANM-023The Chosen One Pending

"Requires no special condition to Spawn" has no database filter, and the selected Catalyst must stay linked to this Trick.

Decision needed: Define the eligible card filter and what happens to the linked Catalyst if this card leaves or changes control.
ANM-024Blazing Inferno Pending

Volcano ownership, the FIRE bonus scope, stacking, and the self-destruction dependency need explicit wording.

Decision needed: Decide whether Volcano must be controlled by this card's controller and whether the +1000 applies to one field or both.
ANM-025Inferno Dragon Pending

The two named support cards may be allowed on either field or may need to be controlled by Inferno Dragon's controller.

Decision needed: Define whose Volcano and Blazing Inferno satisfy the condition and confirm that the bonus updates immediately.
ANM-026Inferno Takeover Pending

The card takes all face-up FIRE Catalysts until End Phase, which creates zone-capacity and return problems.

Decision needed: Define which cards move when zones are limited and what happens if a returning Catalyst has no open original zone.
ANM-001BToolbox Pending

The eligible hand card, exact payment window, once-per-turn tracking, and missing replacement draw need confirmation.

Decision needed: Decide whether the action only shuffles an Equip Palm Trick or also draws a replacement card.
ANM-002BCurse of the Flame Ghost Pending

"Becomes FIRE" sounds like an Attribute replacement, while "gains Vampire" sounds additive.

Decision needed: Confirm which existing properties are replaced, which are kept, and exactly what is restored when the Equip ends.
ANM-003BPhoenix Soul Pending

The controller chooses one of two modes, and mode 2 must remember which FIRE Catalysts are doubled and later destroyed.

Decision needed: Confirm each mode's cost, selection timing, and whether mode 2 snapshots cards or continuously updates the group.
ANM-000ANewz The Great Watcher Pending

"All Warrior-Type Catalysts" may mean both fields and may include Newz itself.

Decision needed: Choose global versus controller-only scope and confirm whether Newz receives its own +200 Pressure.
ANM-000BDestin The Great Warlord Pending

The Fusion material match, optional hand Spawn, and attack-lock duration need separate engine instructions.

Decision needed: Confirm distinct materials, who chooses the hand Catalyst, and how long that Catalyst cannot attack.
ANM-000CAce The Great Pending

Copying a Catalyst effect may accidentally copy identity, costs, limits, state, or unsupported engine behavior. The printed text has no frequency limit.

Decision needed: Define exactly what is copied, whether costs and restrictions are included, and whether the effect is once per turn.
ANM-037Fusion Zone Pending

The text mixes player and team limits and needs exact material, ownership, zone, and Fusion Deck checks.

Decision needed: Define who may activate it, the per-team clock, how two distinct materials match, and the legal Fusion destination.

What a complete ruling must say

1. Timing
When the action starts, when it resolves, and how long it lasts.
2. Ownership and control
Who owns, controls, chooses, pays, and receives each affected card.
3. Zones and targets
Where cards may come from, where they go, and what happens when a zone is full.
4. Engine result
Costs, limits, counters, Kills, Capture, Chi damage, and the exact testable outcome.
