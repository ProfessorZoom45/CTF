CARRY THE FLAME — FORGE REVIEW SUGGESTIONS
First-batch decision queue | 24 September 2026

1. KEEP THE EIGHT ALIGNMENTS FIXED
Hero, Villain, God, Demi-God, Angel, Demon, Djinn, and Spirit are the
only alignment values. Do not infer a ninth category from legacy GROUP
tags. Alignment on many records is absent in the ZIP and is only a draft.

2. CHECK LARGE LEGACY CLASSES BEFORE ADDING A RACE OR SKILL
The following GROUP tags occur on at least eight Catalyst records in
the 1,613-card snapshot. Counts are tag counts, not proof that a new
canonical class is needed. Review card mechanics and visual concept first.

  Spellcaster 93 -> usually Mage, Witch, or Warlock; classify individually.
  Beast-Warrior 43 -> test Beast versus Hybrid; do not auto-add a race.
  Striker 24 -> candidate skill only if at least eight cards retain a
                coherent distinct combat role after generic renaming.
  Pirate 20 -> candidate skill only if eight or more share mechanics.
  Swordsman 14 -> already allowed on Android; consider adding to Human
                  only if eight or more Human cards truly need it.
  Hunter 10 -> compare existing Predator, Warrior, and Ninja skills.
  Shadow 9 -> likely a motif, not automatically a race or skill.
  AlterUser 15 -> normalize to existing Alter User where race is Alter.
  Gundam 10 -> use existing Giant Robot or Machine; replace franchise tag.
  Saiyan 35 -> franchise-specific tag; recast as an allowed race/skill.
  Cart00n 12 -> recast as existing allowed race/skill, not a new alignment.

Other frequent existing or mappable tags include Warrior 361, Human 84,
Machine 83, Fiend 82, Beast 51, Fairy 42, Gunman 29, Zombie 26,
Ninja 19, Mutant 16, Thunder 14, Pyro 13, Vampire 11, Aqua 11,
Winged Beast 11, Rock 10, Dragon 9, Reptile 8, and Plant 8.
These counts are a triage aid and must not decide taxonomy by themselves.

3. PRIORITIZE CARD NAME GROUPS THAT INTERACT
Review a source family and its lineage/origin tags together. When a
Catalyst, Palm Trick, Concealed Trick, Fusion, or Token references another
name, choose names as one family and audit all exact-name references.
The ZIP's REBUILD_AUDIT reports 955 reference updates and 282 generic
name-family/series references from its previous rebuild. Those existing
generic references need a new audit after choices are final.

4. TOKEN RELATIONSHIPS
13 of 24 Tokens have at least one creator reference detectable by exact
"Token Catalyst #" in card text. The other 11 need manual discovery from
effect wording, lineage, or intended token art. Some Tokens are created
by multiple cards, including ZF1 effects. Approve a shared family name
that makes sense for every creator; do not simply rename by the first
found creator if the others disagree. ZTS-12, ZTS-14, ZTS-17, and ZTS-21
currently have non-ZF1 creators with different proposed myth roots. The
preview flags these conflicts and checks selected Token/creator options.

5. TRICK NAMES
Name Tricks after their actual effect and play interaction. Counter
effects should signal denial; Spawn effects should signal arrival or
return; protection effects should signal warding. Preserve precise
timing, target, cost, and duration when rewriting effects. The five
current choices are machine-assisted first passes, not final editorial
recommendations. Replace weak or repetitive combinations in the editor.

6. ZF1 FORMAT SUGGESTIONS — NAME LOCKED
There are 26 ZF1 records: 13 Catalyst cards and 13 Spell/Trap Tricks.
Do not rename any ZF1 card. Review Catalyst alignment/race/skill against
the final tree. Examples needing creator attention:
  ZF1-1 ReEsE The Great: GROUP includes "Gundam"; Giant Robot is an
         allowed Hero race, while the franchise tag should be retired.
  ZF1-5 Aearth: GROUP includes Elf, but Elf is allowed for Angel or Demon,
         not Hero. The correct alignment needs a creator decision.
  ZF1-7 Serpent: GROUP has Reptile/Snake but no explicit alignment;
         choose Hero or Villain without changing the name.
  ZF1-10 Reaper — Master Swordsman: Human/Swordsman is not a current
         allowed skill combination. Map to Warrior or consider the
         eight-card threshold before adding Human Swordsman.
  ZF1-3 Keep It Moving and ZF1-4 Lightning Strike: both make Token #24;
         decide a shared token identity while preserving ZF1 names.
  ZF1-13 Blazing Inferno and ZF1-14 Inferno Dragon: cross-reference
         Palm Trick #438; repair that reference only after its rename.
All ZF1 tricks need effect/timing review, but the eight Catalyst-only
numeric/classification fields are absent by design.

7. NAMING AND RIGHTS REVIEW
Prefer oldest documented myth roots when meaningfully connected to a
card. Use ancient names and concepts, not a modern franchise's portrayal,
art, costume, prose, or distinctive character version. A historical root
does not make a newly combined full title or its market use automatically
clear. Check selected names for trademark conflicts before public release.
Source links and copyright context are in CTF-FORGE-REVIEW-README.txt.

8. RELEASE GATE AFTER CREATOR CHOICES
Export one decision manifest. Resolve missing names and required branch
fields, approve taxonomy changes only where the eight-card evidence is
real, rebuild .ptcg/.cnl with a complete cross-reference rewrite, and
rerun the ZIP's count/uniqueness/reference checks. Keep this preview's
choices out of playable data until that pass succeeds.
