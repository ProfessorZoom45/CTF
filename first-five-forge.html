      A small rules map

      Draw & Spawn

      Draw one card in Draw Phase. Rank 1–4 Normal Spawns need no Tribute; Rank 6 needs one and Rank 7 needs two.

      Pressure & Counter Pressure

      Pressure attacks. Counter Pressure protects a defending Catalyst. Compare the right numbers before declaring a battle.

      Fusion & Tricks

      Fusion follows exact printed material requirements. Palm and Concealed Tricks have different timing and placement.

      Three win paths

      Reduce opposing Chi to zero, reach seven Kills, or reach seven Extractions. The tutorial starts with the core turn loop.
